Danger Zone

Delete workload – in namespace –
Removes the Deployment or DaemonSet resource entirely — its pods are terminated and will not come back until the resource is re-applied (e.g. via your CI/CD pipeline). Much more destructive than deleting a single pod.
Delete pod – in namespace –
Terminates just this pod. If it belongs to a Deployment/ReplicaSet, a replacement pod is created automatically — this is the standard way to force-restart a single pod.
